KennyEQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

KennyEQ is an audio equalizer application. This policy explains its on-device information handling, optional permissions and user controls.

Version scope

The detailed implementation descriptions below refer to KennyEQ 1.2.78 (201585), 1.2.79 (201586), 1.2.80 (201587), 1.2.81 (201588), 1.2.82 (201589), and 1.2.83 (201590). This does not mean that these versions are already available through Google Play. Older test releases can differ in their permission and shortcut implementation; they are not represented here as having identical behavior. The previous policy dated September 26, 2026 remains available for reference. Please include your app version when asking about an older release.

For version 1.2.80, the app includes an offline privacy-policy view in Help. It does not change the setup wizard or shortcut behavior. Version 201586 removed the main-screen shortcut-timing control; previously saved timing remains in use.

Local data and diagnostics

These inspected versions do not require a user account and do not include advertising or analytics SDKs. Equalizer settings, profiles, shortcut timing and consent choices are stored locally. Their audio-effect and shortcut processing takes place on the device.

Local diagnostic logging is distinct from background accessibility processing. While the KennyEQ screen handles remote navigation, it can write selected navigation/media key codes and action information to Android diagnostic logs. Service status and errors can also be logged. Saving settings can write the profile/package identifier and settings values to these local Android diagnostic logs. This is not a claim that no diagnostic data exists. The accessibility shortcut implementation does not log the remote-key codes it processes in the background, or save or transmit its key/window information.

If you choose to email support, your sender address and the information or files you send will be received for handling your request. Do not send passwords or unrelated personal information. Android manages diagnostic-log retention; local app preferences can be removed through Android's app-data controls, which also remove your saved settings.

Optional accessibility shortcut

KennyEQ is an equalizer, not an accessibility tool. Its optional TV remote shortcut uses Android AccessibilityService to open KennyEQ when you briefly press and release OK, then press Down within the configured interval while another app is visible. The service is needed because KennyEQ's own screen cannot receive that sequence when another app is in the foreground.

The service processes remote-key codes and press/release timing locally. It checks window types to suppress the shortcut while an on-screen keyboard is visible. Android grants a window-content capability for this configuration, but these inspected implementations do not read screen text, typed text or passwords. The app does not use accessibility events to select a profile based on the foreground app; the shortcut opens the global equalizer.

In-app consent and separate Android accessibility permission are required. You can decline and use the app icon instead. To turn off an existing Android grant, disable KennyEQ in Android Accessibility settings; declining an in-app prompt does not revoke an earlier Android grant. Shortcut keys are not consumed, so the underlying app may also respond.

Audio and foreground operation

KennyEQ retains user-enabled Android audio-effect controllers for supported playback sessions from other apps. It does not itself play media. Effects depend on the device and supported session; not all audio is guaranteed to be processed. The foreground service and its ongoing notification support this operation. The app's Fully close control stops the equalizer service.

On supported non-TV devices, a separate optional foreground service maintains a visible floating shortcut while other apps are displayed. This service is not media playback and is intentionally skipped on Android TV. Display-over-other-apps permission is used for this optional overlay.

Audio visualization uses Android Visualizer to process playback spectrum information locally. The Android RECORD_AUDIO permission name does not mean that the visualizer records microphone audio. The inspected visualization implementation does not create or upload audio recordings.

Other device permissions

  • Modify audio settings: supports the equalizer's audio-effect functions.
  • Notifications: shows ongoing service notifications where supported and permitted.
  • App and audio-session information: may be used locally for compatible audio-session and profile functions. This is separate from the accessibility shortcut and does not establish foreground-app tracking by that service.
  • System settings and battery optimization requests: are presented for optional features that need these settings for reliable operation. You decide whether to grant them.

Data handling, retention and deletion

In these inspected versions, normal EQ and shortcut operation does not routinely transmit settings, key/window information or visualization data to the developer or another service. Support email and independently visiting the published policy website are separate. A website visit connects to GitHub Pages and may expose ordinary request information such as an IP address to the hosting provider; opening the bundled policy does not make that visit.

In the inspected versions, settings and consent are held in app-local storage. No billing, subscription-validation, license-server or Android ID processing was identified in this implementation. This statement does not establish the behavior of older Google Play builds. The Help policy view is bundled and does not send data to a website when opened.

Local settings remain until changed, cleared through Android app-data controls, or removed with the app. Diagnostic logs are managed by Android. Support email is handled through the developer's email provider; correspondence may include the sender address, message and attachments. Contact the address below to request deletion of correspondence. A fixed mailbox retention period has not been established in this policy, and email-provider retention and backups may also apply.

Data security

Settings and consent are stored in Android app-private preferences. The app disables Android backup and configures exclusions of its app data from cloud backup and device transfer. Android manages system diagnostic logs; these controls are not a guarantee against all device or operating-system access.

Keep your device and operating system updated and review optional permissions in Android settings. This policy does not promise identical behavior for unaudited older builds or guarantee that all device diagnostics are absent.

Children

KennyEQ is not directed to children.
