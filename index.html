KennyEQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

KennyEQ is an audio equalizer application. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information and device permissions.

Data collection and sharing

KennyEQ does not require an account and does not collect, sell, or share personal information with third parties. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Device permissions

KennyEQ may request device permissions only to provide its audio and user-interface features:

  • Microphone/audio access: used locally for audio visualization and equalizer-related functions. Audio is not recorded, uploaded, or shared.
  • Modify audio settings and foreground media service: used to apply equalizer settings while audio plays.
  • Notifications: used to show the active equalizer service notification.
  • Display over other apps: used only when an optional equalizer overlay is enabled by the user.
  • Accessibility service: used only after the user enables it, to support the app's optional audio-control workflow. It is not used to collect, transmit, or share personal data.
  • Installed-app visibility: used locally to identify compatible audio applications and sessions. The app list is not transmitted or shared.
  • System settings and battery optimization requests: used only when the user chooses features that need these settings for reliable audio operation.

Data security

Audio-processing and device-control operations are performed locally on the device. KennyEQ does not operate a server for collecting user information.

Children

KennyEQ is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app features or legal requirements change. The latest version will be available at this page.
